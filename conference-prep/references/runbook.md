# Conference Prep Runbook

## Intake

Capture:

- Conference name and acronym
- Dates, timezone, city, venue
- Registration status and attendee-app access status
- Company context: product, sales, investor, customer, recruiting, personal brand, or other
- Success criteria: meetings booked, customer discovery, investor relationships, speaking prep, market intelligence, hiring, partnerships
- Constraints: travel, deck deadline, approvals needed, no-contact list, sensitive topics

## Timeline

T-30 to T-14:

- Gather official site, agenda, speakers, sponsors, attendee list if available.
- Build account map and identify target categories.
- Check prior memory and CRM for existing relationships.
- Start deck gap list.

T-14 to T-7:

- Enrich priority contacts.
- Draft outreach by relationship tier.
- Build meeting scheduler and suggested slots.
- Create pitch deck brief and demo notes.

T-7 to T-1:

- Confirm logistics and registration.
- Finalize pocket brief.
- Draft messages, calendar holds, and meeting prep notes.
- Prepare notes capture files for mobile use.

During event:

- Capture every useful conversation as raw notes first.
- Tag each note: `hot-lead`, `warm`, `partner`, `investor`, `press`, `intel`, `follow-up`, `no-action`.
- Add exact quotes, objections, and promised actions.
- Update scheduler status at least once daily.

T+0 to T+2:

- Convert raw notes into follow-up queue.
- Draft personalized follow-ups within 48 hours.
- Update CRM and opportunity records.
- Write debrief: top signals, pipeline impact, missed targets, next event improvements.

## Target Segmentation

- Customer/prospect: likely buyer, budget holder, user, or strategic account.
- Partner: distribution, implementation, integration, data, ecosystem, or channel leverage.
- Investor/capital: relationship and market thesis, not a hard raise unless explicitly requested.
- Press/content: useful for public narrative, founder visibility, podcast, newsletter, or social.
- Competitor/intel: learn positioning, pricing, buyer objections, workflows, and market language.
- Internal/logistics: travel, booth, team coordination, deck, collateral, registration.

## Pitch Deck Prep

Create a deck brief before touching slides:

- Audience: who will see it and what they care about.
- Job-to-be-done: why this event creates a buying or partnership moment.
- Core thesis: one sentence.
- Proof: customer logos, metrics, case studies, founder credibility, demos.
- Event-specific angle: why this matters now, in this room.
- Objections: compliance, data access, cost, implementation, accuracy, trust.
- Ask: meeting, pilot, intro, data-sharing, investment, partnership.
- Missing assets: screenshots, demo clips, diagrams, pricing, one-pager, security docs.

## Meeting Scheduler

Required fields:

- Priority
- Name
- Company
- Role
- Category
- Relationship
- Desired meeting outcome
- Best channel
- Draft message status
- Suggested slot
- Confirmed slot
- Owner
- Notes
- Follow-up due

## Contact Enrichment

Use source order:

1. Existing CRM/Shuttle, Google Sheets, prior conference docs.
2. Calendar, Gmail, Fireflies, meeting notes, and memory search.
3. Beeper/WhatsApp/iMessage only when relationship context matters.
4. LinkedIn/public web, company site, press, X/Bird where relevant.
5. Attendee app or private list only if the user/client has legitimate access or provides export/screenshots.

For each enriched contact, record:

- Source and date checked
- Relationship strength
- Last interaction
- Business priority
- Personal or contextual opener
- Risk/caution notes
- Recommended next action

## Follow-up Flow

- Same day: raw capture and triage.
- 24 hours: hot leads and promised sends.
- 48 hours: warm reconnects and informational follows.
- 72 hours: CRM cleanup and sequence drafts.
- 7 days: debrief scorecard and next-event improvements.
